BIOGRAPHY

Professional background

Dr. Hakkı İzmirli completed medical school at Istanbul University and specialist training in aesthetic and plastic surgery at Kocaeli University. Following practice in Derince, Sakarya, Levent and Fulya, he continues his work at his clinic in Nişantaşı, Istanbul.

After specialist training, Dr. İzmirli completed compulsory service at Derince Training and Research Hospital. He subsequently worked in Sakarya, Levent and Fulya before establishing his current Nişantaşı practice.
