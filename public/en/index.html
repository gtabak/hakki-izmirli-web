Revision rhinoplasty

Breast augmentation may use implants or other techniques where clinically suitable to change breast volume and shape. Implant-related risks, follow-up and individual anatomy require careful discussion.

QUESTIONS

Common questions

Individual examination is essential before any surgical decision.

Can I discuss my case before visiting Istanbul?+

Contact the clinic to ask how an initial discussion and review of your medical history may be arranged. A definitive treatment recommendation requires appropriate medical evaluation.

How long should I stay in Istanbul?+

This depends on the procedure, health status and recovery. Confirm any travel plans and fitness to fly with your treating medical team.

Do all patients qualify for surgery?+

No. The suitability of a procedure depends on individual medical factors, expectations and clinical findings.
