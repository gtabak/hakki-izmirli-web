Information for international patients

International visitors should plan around medical suitability, clinical follow-up and safe recovery rather than fixed travel packages.

TRAVEL & CARE

Prepare for a clinical visit to Istanbul.

Before booking travel, discuss your goals, medical history, practical questions and appropriate follow-up directly with the clinic. Surgery and travel suitability require individual clinical assessment.

01

Initial enquiry

Provide relevant medical history and medicines

02

Clinical assessment

Ask about the examination and any investigations required

03

Planning & consent

Discuss possible complications and alternatives

04

Follow-up and recovery

Agree on post-procedure care and safe travel timing

Individual examination is essential before any surgical decision.
